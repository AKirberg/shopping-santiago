---
name: Recommender routes
description: Rule for creating multi-mall routes from the recommender.
---

When a visitor has a half day or full day, the recommender lets them select two or more ranked malls and opens a Google Maps route from their confirmed location through those stops.

**Why:** Longer available time supports a shopping itinerary, not only a single-destination recommendation.

**How to apply:** Keep the route option limited to long-duration choices, require at least two selected malls, and use the confirmed location plus verified mall coordinates in the route link.
