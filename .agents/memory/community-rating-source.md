---
name: Community rating source
description: Product decision on which visitor review scores may be shown.
---

Mall ratings shown to visitors must be calculated only from reviews submitted through Shopeando. Do not fetch, display, combine, or compare Google Maps ratings with the community score.

**Why:** The owner wants the ranking to grow from the Shopeando community rather than importing a third-party score.

**How to apply:** Keep map destinations available when useful, but any score, count, average, ranking, or review summary visible in the product must use the Shopeando review API only.
